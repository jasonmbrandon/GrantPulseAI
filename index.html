1. Interactive Grant Jargon Decoder

SBIR vs. STTR Federal Programs

SBIR (Small Business Innovation Research) requires the Principal Investigator (PI) to be >51% employed by the small business. STTR (Technology Transfer) requires formal partnership with a research institution (e.g. university lab).

SAM.gov UEI & CAGE Code Entity Registration

UEI (Unique Entity ID) is a 12-character alphanumeric code assigned by SAM.gov. CAGE Code is assigned by the DLA for federal contract and grant payment authorization.

NIH 5-Criterion Rubric Reviewer Scoring

NIH study sections evaluate proposals across 5 core criteria: Significance, Investigator, Innovation, Approach, and Environment.

Indirect Cost / F&A Rate Budget Strategy

Federal grants allow small businesses to claim a standard 40% de minimis Indirect Cost Rate on direct labor to fund company overhead, rent, and non-direct software expenses.
